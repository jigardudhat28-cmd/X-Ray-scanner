About the system

What is the AI X-Ray Scanner System?
A web application that analyses a chest X-ray image with a trained deep-learning model and reports whether it looks Normal or shows signs of Pneumonia.
How are AI and CNN used?
A Convolutional Neural Network learns visual patterns, such as edges, textures and opacities in the lungs, from thousands of labelled X-rays. This project uses transfer learning: MobileNetV2, pretrained on ImageNet, is fine-tuned on a public chest X-ray dataset.
What does it detect?
Two classes only: Normal and Pneumonia. It does not detect any other disease.
How does the analysis work?
  • The uploaded image is checked to make sure it looks like a chest X-ray.
  • It is resized to 224 x 224 pixels and normalised.
  • The CNN outputs a probability for Pneumonia; the Normal probability is the remainder.
  • The higher probability becomes the prediction, shown with its confidence.
Purpose
An M.Sc. AI & ML Semester 3 academic project demonstrating deep learning in medical image classification.
Technology used
Python, Flask, TensorFlow/Keras, NumPy, Pillow, scikit-learn, SQLite, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
Limitations
  • Trained on one public dataset; results may not carry over to other hospitals or X-ray machines.
  • The image check is a simple rule-based filter and can occasionally accept or reject the wrong image.
  • Confidence scores are model outputs, not clinical certainty.
Academic demo only. This system is not a medical device and is not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified doctor.
